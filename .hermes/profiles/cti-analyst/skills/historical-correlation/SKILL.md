# Historical correlation

Search exact identities first: canonical URL, advisory ID, CVE, campaign,
product/version, IOC, malware/tool, and ATT&CK ID. Then review candidate
relationships and contradictions. Label each result deterministic, sourced
assertion, or inference; include direction, confidence, evidence IDs, and
justification. Submit proposals through the supported service interface only.
